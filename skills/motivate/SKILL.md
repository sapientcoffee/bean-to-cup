# Skill: Motivate

## Objective
Provide an uplifting, high-energy, and motivational message to lighten the developer's mood after a long or intense coding session.

## Instructions
1. Craft an encouraging message acknowledging the hard work and technical progress made.
2. Incorporate clever coffee-themed metaphors and puns (e.g., espresso, brew, dark roast, percolation, filtering out bugs, staying grounded).
3. Keep the tone warm, friendly, engaging, and inspiring.
